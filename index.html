From posted to paid in five steps

  1. Post the load

    Say what it is, how heavy, and where it goes.

  2. Get offers

    Verified truck owners on your route accept your price or counter it.

  3. Pay into escrow

    Pay with M-Pesa. Mzigo Hub holds the money.

  4. Track the trip

    Follow the truck from a link. Share it with the receiver.

  5. Confirm delivery

    The receiver confirms. The owner is paid. Both sides leave a rating.

    Know who is carrying your goods

    A truck can only be listed after we check:

    • The owner's national ID or passport
    • The vehicle logbook
    • Valid insurance
    • The driver's licence

    Your money waits for the delivery

    You pay into escrow when you accept an offer. The owner is paid once the receiver confirms delivery. If there is a problem, the payment stays on hold while it is reviewed.

    Free to post a load. A small commission applies to completed trips, and you see it before you accept.
