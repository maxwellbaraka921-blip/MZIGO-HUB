What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your name, phone or WhatsApp number, country, whether you are a shipper, truck owner or driver, and the route or fleet details you type in.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about Mzigo Hub, such as telling you when your route opens. We do not sell your details.

Who sees it

The Mzigo Hub team, and the form or email service used to deliver your message to us.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@mzigohub.com.
