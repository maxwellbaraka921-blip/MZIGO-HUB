Mzigo Hub

Privacy note

Draft. It must be reviewed by a lawyer, and Mzigo Hub registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya, before real accounts and documents are collected.

What we collect

On the waitlist: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, country, role and the route or fleet details you type in.

If you create an account: your email, your full name, your M-Pesa phone number, and whether you ship or carry cargo.

If you list a truck: its number plate and capacity, and photos or PDFs of the owner's ID or passport, the vehicle logbook, insurance and the driver's licence.

If you post or carry loads: the load details, the receiver's name and phone number, and payment records such as M-Pesa receipt numbers and amounts.

Why we collect it

To run Mzigo Hub: to check that trucks and owners are real, match loads to trucks, take payment, pay truck owners, handle problems, and contact you about your account. We do not sell your details.

Who sees it

The Mzigo Hub team. Truck documents are seen only by staff who verify trucks. After a customer has paid, the customer and the truck owner see each other's name and phone number for the trip, and the owner sees the receiver's name and number. Safaricom receives the phone numbers and amounts needed to process M-Pesa payments. Google (Firebase) stores the data for us, and Formspree delivers waitlist messages.

How long we keep it

Account and payment records are kept as long as the law requires for business and tax records. Truck documents are kept while the truck is listed and removed on request after that, unless we must keep them for a dispute or the law.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@mzigohub.com. Your name, number and role are fixed after sign-up for payment safety, so changes go through us.

This website

The site remembers your chosen country in your browser so it opens in the right currency. It does not use advertising trackers. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
